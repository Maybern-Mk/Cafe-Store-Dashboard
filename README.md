# Café Sales & Performance Analysis (Power BI)

## Overview  
This Power BI project analyzes café sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends to identify key drivers of revenue and operational efficiency.  

The dashboard is designed to support business decision-making by providing clear insights into sales patterns, popular items, peak hours, and overall performance.

---

## Objectives  
- Analyze overall café sales performance  
- Identify top-selling products and categories  
- Understand customer purchasing patterns  
- Detect peak sales hours and busy days  
- Provide actionable insights for inventory and staffing decisions  

---

## Tools and Technologies  
- **Power BI**  
- **Power Query** for data cleaning and transformation  
- **DAX** for calculated measures and KPIs

## Dashboard Features  

### Key KPIs  
- Total Sales Revenue  
- Total Orders  
- Average Order Value  
- Daily and Monthly Sales Trends  

### Visualizations  
- Bar charts for top-selling menu items  
- Category-wise sales comparison  
- Time-based analysis (hourly and daily trends)  

### Filters and Interactivity  
- Date  
- Product category  
- Item name  

---

## Key Insights  
- A small group of products contributes significantly to total revenue  
- Distinct peak hours show the highest sales volume  
- Certain product categories consistently outperform others  
- Sales trends provide guidance for inventory restocking and staff scheduling

## Business Value

- Improves inventory planning  
- Enables data-driven staff allocation  
- Identifies opportunities for promotions and pricing strategies
